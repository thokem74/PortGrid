# PortGrid

PortGrid models container transfers among vessel berths, yard blocks, and the truck gate.

## Language

**Transport order**:
An instruction to move a specific container from its source to its destination.

**Live job**:
A handling or transport job with physical work outstanding, including a job whose assigned vehicle is prepositioning.

**Yard slot reservation**:
A live job's claim on a yard storage position for its current operation, a relay handover, or final delivery.
